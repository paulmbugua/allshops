# Pilot launch checklist

- [ ] Business details, category, currency and timezone verified.
- [ ] Trial/plan and subscription state confirmed.
- [ ] Branch, users, roles and POS device configured.
- [ ] Catalogue and opening stock verified through normal ledger workflows.
- [ ] Receipt, barcode scanner and payment procedures tested.
- [ ] Reports and reconciliation reviewed.
- [ ] Offline workflow tested where the flag is enabled.
- [ ] UAT checklist completed and support process explained.
- [ ] Backup and production health confirmed.
- [ ] Platform admin activates the pilot with readiness evidence.

The initial cohort is 5–10 Qatar businesses, normally 1–3 users, one primary branch and 1–2 POS devices each.
